# Our Love Story — Version 7

Open index.html in Safari or Chrome. You can share the entire folder via a web host later.

## Characters — unchanged
The two source PNGs under assets/fizz-original.png and assets/arthi-original.png are **unaltered** copies of the exact illustrations you uploaded. The page only animates the images with CSS.

## Photo gallery
Add .jpg photos inside assets/memories as described in ADD_YOUR_PHOTOS_HERE.txt. Image slots will appear as photos automatically when you reload. Tap any photo to view it full-screen; swipe is not required, use the previous/next buttons or keyboard arrows.

## Birthday video
Save your video as assets/birthday-message.mp4 (H.264 MP4 works well on iPhone/Safari). After opening the birthday letter and clicking the reveal button, the video will show. Until then the site displays a clear placeholder.

## Marriage
Final chapter follows the birthday surprise. No confirmed marriage date is assumed.

## Offline
Core website works without a server or external JavaScript. Google Fonts may fall back to local serif/sans fonts offline.

## Version 9 — The soundtrack
- Your supplied MP3 is included at `assets/one-day-the-distance-ends.mp3`.
- When Arthi opens the page, she taps “Begin with music” to start the soundtrack. Browsers do not allow reliable sound-on autoplay before interaction.
- “Continue quietly” is also available. A floating play/pause button and volume slider remain available as she scrolls.
- The background track loops and pauses during any playing video (including birthday and gallery videos), then attempts to resume afterward.
- Music is embedded locally, so no YouTube stream or internet connection is needed for it.
- For public hosting/sharing, make sure you have permission to distribute the song.
