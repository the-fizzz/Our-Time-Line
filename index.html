When the distance ends ♡

My Bibijan, One day, this distance will only be a memory we look back on together. One day, there will be no airport goodbyes, no time difference, and no waiting for a screen to light up. When that day comes, I promise to keep choosing you in the little things and the big things — in quiet mornings, in hard days, in laughter, in prayer, and in every dream we build side by side. I promise that our love story will not end with distance. It will become a home, a marriage, and a life we finally get to share together. Until then, keep this promise with you: I am still walking toward our forever, and I will keep walking until I reach you.
Forever yours, Fizz ♡
